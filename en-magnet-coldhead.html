Magnet Service & Coldheads

We replace your coldheads promptly, economically and with warranty.

Magnet service leaves no room for error and requires experience, training and professionalism. With our ramp and shim power supplies, coldhead replacement kits and other magnet service equipment, we provide the complete service from a single source.

We supply coldheads, compressors and adsorbers for your MR systems from stock as quickly as possible, with warranty, and our trained team performs the replacements. Call us for liquid helium fills as well.

Scope of service

  • Coldhead replacement
  • Helium compressor service & replacement
  • Adsorber replacement
  • Helium & nitrogen refill
  • Ramp-up & shimming
  • Magnet precool
  • Leak check
  • Ice removal
  • Gradient coil replacement
  • Recommissioning after quench
  • Magnet cooling
  • RF room installation

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a coldhead be replaced?

Coldhead life depends on the system model, operating conditions and the condition of the cooling system. During periodic maintenance we monitor coldhead and compressor performance and plan the replacement in advance.

How long is the system down during replacement?

It depends on the system model and the condition of the magnet. We plan the work in advance to minimise downtime and state the estimated duration in our quotation.

Do you provide support after a quench?

Yes. Cooling the magnet, helium fill, ramp-up and shimming to bring it back into service after a quench are part of our services.
