MR Coils

Warranted supply and repair of MR coils for GE and all other brands.

We supply coils for GE and all other brands of magnetic resonance systems economically and with warranty.

We also repair all types of coils for your systems.

Scope of service

  • Coil supply – GE and all other brands
  • Coil repair – all coil types
  • Testing & fault diagnosis
  • Accessories

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a faulty coil be repaired?

In most cases, yes. We test the faulty coil in our workshop and report whether repair or replacement is more economical.

What is the warranty period?

The coils we supply and repair are covered by warranty. The warranty period is stated in our quotation.
