Frequently Asked Questions

Which brands and systems do you service?

We service MR, CT, PET/CT, gamma camera, angio and X-ray systems and workstations — above all GE (General Electric), as well as Siemens, Philips and Canon (Toshiba). See Systems We Support for the full model list.

How quickly do you respond to a breakdown?

Our response time depends on the type of maintenance contract for your system and is clearly defined in the contract. Customers under contract receive 24/7 emergency service support.

Do you provide remote support?

Yes. We start troubleshooting right away by phone and, where the system supports it, connect remotely for a preliminary diagnosis. This allows a faster and more accurate response.

Do you provide a report after service?

Yes. We hand over a service report detailing all work performed and, for periodic maintenance, a maintenance checklist.

Do you service systems out of warranty?

Yes. We provide independent maintenance and repair for systems whose manufacturer warranty has expired.

What is the difference between contracts with and without parts?

With parts, spare part costs are covered by the contract. Without parts, periodic maintenance and labor are covered; parts are quoted separately.

How long is a maintenance contract and how is it billed?

Our maintenance contracts are annual and invoiced monthly, so you can plan your maintenance budget evenly throughout the year.

How fast are spare parts delivered?

Spare parts in our stock are shipped from our warehouses in Türkiye and Germany on the same business day your order is placed, keeping your system's downtime to a minimum.

Do spare parts come with a warranty?

Yes. The spare parts we supply are covered by warranty in line with local regulations and policies.

Do used systems come with a warranty?

Yes. All our used systems are delivered in full working order; this is our basic warranty. Any warranty period and scope beyond that is agreed together under the terms of the sale or rental agreement.

Do you offer consultancy when buying a system?

Yes. We carry out performance and quality tests on the system you are considering, as an expert inspection, so you can invest with confidence.

Do you provide service abroad?

Yes. Outside Türkiye we have worked in — Europe: Germany, France, Italy, Greece, Cyprus; Russia, the Caucasus and Central Asia: Russia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan; Middle East: Iraq, United Arab Emirates; Africa: Algeria, Sudan; Americas: USA — including turnkey relocation and installation projects.
